      A little guide.

      Outside First helps you choose one ordinary thing to notice in a familiar outdoor place. Its 20 activity cards are written in advance; the model ranks them from your words.

      Preparation loads an open sentence encoder from this app’s server. Inference runs here, in a browser worker. Your preference text, notes, and choices are not sent to a remote inference service.

      Field notes and preferences use this app’s own local browser storage. Clearing this site’s storage removes them. There is no account, location request, tracking, or notification.

      Model & offline details

      Model: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, Apache-2.0.
      Pinned revision: 751bff37182d3f1213fa05d7196b954e230abad9.
      Runtime: Transformers.js with local ONNX WebAssembly.

      The model compares sentence embeddings; it does not write or invent a mission. Time and staying in one place determine which cards are eligible.

      “Keep for offline use” caches an explicit list of this app’s files, model, and runtime on this device. The app reports whether that cache preparation succeeds. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; keep your app server available until preparation succeeds.
